HERITAGE & EXCELLENCE

DIMORE Aluminium Series – The Informed Choice

DIMORE by Qutone Ceramic is one of India's most reputable manufacturers of high-performance aluminium doors and windows. We are continually pushing the boundaries of technology in order to provide you with a diverse range of world-class and accessible architectural solutions.

DIMORE leaves no stone unturned in making your experience rich, whether it's for luxury residential homes or expansive commercial developments. As an organization, we make every effort to ensure transparency, rigor, and technical precision in all our operations.

"Every product undergoes a rigorous multi-stage manufacturing and conformance testing protocol, exceeding both national and international architectural standards."

Our systems are reinforced in structural ways to guarantee they remain rigid, quiet, and durable across decades. We combine advanced German engineering with seamless aesthetics, creating a living environment that communicates harmoniously with nature.

PHILOSOPHY & DESIGN

The Spirit of Architecture

System windows and doors are the spirit of modern architecture. DIMORE allows the soul of a structure to communicate directly with nature. Through professional vision and creative design, our engineers build a seamless bridge between man, space, and the environment.

Our comprehensive portfolio includes sliding windows, fixed pane panorama systems, projected casement and sash windows, multi-panel sliding-folding doors, and double-glazed acoustic barriers.

"By balancing geography, weather dynamics, and thermal efficiency, DIMORE delivers uncompromised acoustic dampening, security, and effortless ease of operation."

Featuring fixed panes, top-opening fanlights, and side-hung configurations, DIMORE casement and sliding systems can be combined to create an almost limitless range of bespoke styles for both new luxury builds and retro-fit renovations.
