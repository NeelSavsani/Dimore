ARCHITECTURAL SPECIFICATION

Vista • Contemporary Style

Vista series is created and designed in contemporary styles to complement your desires and taste. A sliding window from Dimore by Qutone can be the most exquisite choice for your home decor. They can be used in residential and commercial structures, adding a beautiful visual element while also serving a practical purpose. With sliding window systems, you may enclose big openings, create a sunroom, or customize a shopfront.

Features & Benefits

  • Sleek sightline 25 mm for maximum visual transparency.
  • Versatile and durable, highly resilient against extreme temperatures.
  • Smooth movement ensured by high-quality nylon rollers.
  • Almost completely maintenance free for a long service life thanks to minimum wear.
  • Eco-friendly, crafted from sustainable architectural aluminium.
  • Scalable sizing, allowing designs up to 10 feet in height.
  • Premium materials including branded genuine glass & best quality imported hardware.
  • Complete customization in terms of size, glass, and color.
  • Optional insect screen available for enhanced utility.

SYSTEM CONFIGURATIONS

Vista Window Styles

Modular track geometries adapted for every architectural opening.

2 Track 2 Shutter Sliding

Classic double-track design offering 50% clear opening. Ideal for bedrooms, balconies, and standard residential apertures.

3 Track 3 Shutter Sliding

Triple-track system sliding seamlessly behind one another to open 66% of the aperture for wide panoramic views.

2 Track Aluminium Adjustable Louvers

Integrated aerofoil louver blades for privacy and continuous natural ventilation without compromising weatherproofing.

3 Track with Integrated Bug Mesh

Dedicated third track housing high-tensile SS 304 mosquito barrier mesh for uninterrupted insect-free fresh airflow.

BESPOKE ARCHITECTURE

Your Window. Your Style.

A home is the reflection of those who reside in it. With Dimore, you have total freedom to customize your Dura Slim system with premium finishes, specialized glazing, and bespoke hardware.

GLASS

Toughened safety glass, Low-E thermal coatings, acoustic laminated soundproof glass, and designer tinted options.

GLAZING
