SIGNATURE SLIDING SERIES

DURA SLIM

Ultra-minimalist interlock sliding system engineered to maximize natural illumination, blur boundaries between indoors and outdoors, and elevate luxury architectural living.

40mm Minimal Interlock German Tech Virgin Aluminium
Interlock Sightline 40 mm
Wind Resistance 3.5 kPa
Track Option 2 & 3 Track
ARCHITECTURAL SPECIFICATION

Duraslim System • Minimalist Luxury

Duraslim system, which features the slimmest interlock design available, excellent for residential and commercial projects that require space efficiency while still providing simplicity and a slim design. An excellent choice for residential homes, villas, and apartment flats, giving spectacular and functional results.

Features & Benefits

  • Easy to operate and maintenance-free with high-precision ball bearing roller guides.
  • Establishes a seamless flow between interior spaces and expansive outdoor views.
  • Easy gliding function that saves valuable room space compared to conventional swing doors.
  • Allows ample natural light to flood interiors through ultra-slim vertical interlock profiles.
  • Facilitates proper ventilation with flexible lateral sash positioning and multi-stop latching.
  • Protects from air and noise pollution via continuous EPDM multi-chamber airtight weather seals.
  • Branded genuine glass & best quality imported hardware manufactured to European standards.
  • Eco-friendly option crafted from 100% sustainable, infinitely recyclable virgin architectural aluminium.
  • Energy-efficient thermal barriers that dramatically reduce air conditioning loads and heat gain.

SYSTEM CONFIGURATIONS

Dura Slim Window Styles

Modular track geometries adapted for every architectural opening.

2 Track 2 Shutter Sliding

Classic double-track design offering 50% clear opening. Ideal for bedrooms, balconies, and standard residential apertures.

3 Track 3 Shutter Sliding

Triple-track system sliding seamlessly behind one another to open 66% of the aperture for wide panoramic views.

2 Track Aluminium Adjustable Louvers

Integrated aerofoil louver blades for privacy and continuous natural ventilation without compromising weatherproofing.

3 Track with Integrated Bug Mesh

Dedicated third track housing high-tensile SS 304 mosquito barrier mesh for uninterrupted insect-free fresh airflow.

BESPOKE ARCHITECTURE

Your Window. Your Style.

A home is the reflection of those who reside in it. With Dimore, you have total freedom to customize your Dura Slim system with premium finishes, specialized glazing, and bespoke hardware.

GLASS

Toughened safety glass, Low-E thermal coatings, acoustic laminated soundproof glass, and designer tinted options.

GLAZING

Single glazing or high-performance Double Glazed Units (DGU) with warm-edge spacers filled with insulating Argon gas.

HARDWARE

German multi-point locksets, concealed flush pulls, designer ergonomic handles, and heavy-duty tandem stainless rollers.

COLOUR & FINISH

Architectural powder coating in 150+ RAL colors, natural anodized metallic sheens, and hyper-realistic wood grain textures.

MESH

SS 304 security bug mesh woven for superior transparency, air circulation, and long-term rust-free corrosion resistance.
